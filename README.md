My ex, in exactly one word: **Unsubscribed**.

Like a newsletter I never even signed up for — one click, no more updates,
and somehow they STILL keep landing in the spam folder. I didn't block them.
I just opted out of the whole relationship.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes that single word to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — nothing else attached.
